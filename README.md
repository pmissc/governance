# PMISSC Governance

This repository is the central home for governance materials of the Project Management Institute Silver Spring Chapter (PMISSC). It organizes approved and working policies, procedures, standards, templates, decisions, and archived material.

Review [Repository Security and Information-Handling Rules](SECURITY.md) before adding material. Credentials, raw tenant exports, and populated access inventories do not belong in Git.

## Repository sections

- [Policies](policies/): Board-approved rules and organizational requirements.
- [Procedures](procedures/): Repeatable instructions for carrying out chapter work.
- [Standards](standards/): Mandatory specifications and conventions.
- [Templates](templates/): Reusable forms and document starting points.
- [Decisions](decisions/): Recorded governance and architectural decisions.
- [Archive](archive/): Superseded or inactive material retained for reference.

## Document management

Each document should identify its owner, approval status, effective date, version, and review cycle where applicable. Superseded material should be moved to the archive rather than deleted when it has continuing historical or compliance value.
